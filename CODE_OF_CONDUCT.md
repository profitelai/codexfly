# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Codexfly to be a respectful, technically serious, and welcoming open-source community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- discuss ideas directly without personal attacks
- assume good intent while still challenging weak reasoning
- keep technical discussions focused and useful

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- abusive language
- discrimination
- bad-faith disruption
- publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness problematic behavior, open a private maintainer contact channel when available.
