# CEO Review

Summarize Codexfly work for stakeholders.

## Instructions

1. Focus on business value, strategic fit, progress, blockers, and next decisions.
2. Keep the tone crisp and executive-friendly.
3. Write to `docs/executive-reviews/<slug>.md` when requested.
4. Avoid deep implementation detail unless it changes delivery risk or ROI.

## Output sections

- Executive summary
- Why it matters
- Progress
- Risks or blockers
- Recommended decisions
- Next 1 to 3 actions
